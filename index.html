Your Four Pillars

BaZi reads your birth as four pairs — Year, Month, Day, and Hour — each pair a Heavenly Stem over an Earthly Branch. Each character carries one of five elements (Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water), and the mix across all eight is what the rest of this reading is based on.

Five Element Balance

The Five Elements (Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water) sit in a cycle: each one generates the next (Wood feeds Fire, Fire leaves Earth as ash, Earth bears Metal, Metal collects Water, Water nourishes Wood) and controls the one two steps ahead (Wood breaks Earth, Earth dams Water, Water douses Fire, Fire melts Metal, Metal chops Wood). Everything below is built on that cycle.

The bars count each of your eight chart characters — the Heavenly Stem and Earthly Branch of all four pillars — by which element it belongs to. For simplicity, this reading counts only each branch's dominant element; branches actually carry one to three "hidden stems" (藏干) with secondary elements too, which a full professional reading weighs in as well.

A tall bar isn't "good" and a short one isn't "bad" on its own — what matters is how each element relates to your Day Master, which is what the rest of this panel works out.
